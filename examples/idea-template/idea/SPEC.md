# Pantry Pilot

A tiny web app for deciding what to cook from ingredients already in the kitchen. Keep it cheerful, quick, and useful on a phone while standing in front of the fridge.

## Capture what I have

Let me add an ingredient with a name, rough quantity, and optional use-by date. The common case should take one hand and no more than a few seconds.

Show today's ingredients as large cards. Put anything expiring soon at the front without making the screen feel alarming.

## Suggest dinner

Offer three dinner ideas that use as many listed ingredients as possible. Each idea needs a short explanation of why it fits and may name up to two missing staples.

I want a “surprise me” action when I do not feel like comparing options.

## Make the result easy to follow

Turn a chosen dinner into a compact cooking view with ordered steps, ingredient quantities, and a keep-awake toggle. It should be readable from across a kitchen counter.
